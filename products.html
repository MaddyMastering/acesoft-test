Smart Solutions for a
Smarter Learning Ecosystem

Empowering the Future of Learning

We empower educational institutions, students, and parents with purpose-built digital platforms that simplify administration, streamline communication, and enrich the overall learning experience. From enrollment and classroom management to progress tracking and real-time parent engagement, our solutions are designed to make education more accessible, transparent, and impactful.

Cloud-Powered ERP for Educational Institutions

Edukul is a comprehensive cloud-based ERP platform designed to digitize and automate core operations of schools, colleges, and training institutes. It helps institutions manage academics, administration, finance, and communication with efficiency and transparency.


Key highlights

Student lifecycle management (admissions to alumni)

Attendance, timetable, exam, and report card automation

Staff, payroll, and HR modules

Real-time dashboards and analytics for decision-makers

Mobile-friendly access for parents, teachers, and students

Simplifying School Discovery and Admissions

Edvantage Point is a smart online platform connecting parents with K-12 schools across India. It makes school search and application effortless, while helping institutions attract, engage, and convert prospective students.


For Parents

  • Discover top schools with filters by location, board, and fees
  • Apply to multiple schools through a single interface
  • Get expert guidance on school selection and career streams

For Schools

  • Digital outreach and brand visibility
  • Centralized admission management and lead tracking
  • Tools to streamline recruitment and parent engagement

Digital Commerce Platform for School Supplies

EdQart simplifies the end-to-end process of buying, selling, and delivering educational materials. Built for speed and accuracy, it serves vendors, institutions, and parents with a seamless shopping experience.


Platform Features

Catalogs for books, uniforms, stationery, and digital tools

Multi-vendor support with centralized order management

Inventory tracking and real-time order updates

Modular architecture with dashboards for schools and suppliers

Personalized Extracurricular Learning for Children

Wizaru is an online learning platform offering live, interactive classes for children aged 3 and above. From coding and STEM to art, music, and public speaking, Wizaru nurtures creativity and confidence through fun, structured learning.


Why Wizaru

Live, one-on-one or group classes by verified instructors

AR-powered learning tools (WizAR flashcards, storybooks, games)

Flexible scheduling and curated learning paths

Progress tracking and feedback for parents
