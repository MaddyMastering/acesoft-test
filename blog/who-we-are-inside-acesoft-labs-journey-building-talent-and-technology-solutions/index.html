Who We Are: Inside Acesoft Labs' Journey Building Talent and Technology Solutions



Every company that grows eventually runs into the same two problems. It needs the right people, and it needs technology that works. Most partners solve one of these. For 16 years, Acesoft Labs has been solving both.

This is our story: who we are, what we do, where we work, and why enterprises across India, the UAE and the USA trust us to build, scale and staff their technology initiatives.

Who Is Acesoft Labs?

Acesoft Labs India Pvt. Ltd. is a technology and staffing partner headquartered in Bengaluru, India. Since 2010, we have helped enterprises access scarce skills, build flexible workforces and deliver technology outcomes.

Today, a team of 300+ professionals works with clients across three regions:

  • India: Acesoft Labs (India) Pvt. Ltd., Bengaluru
  • UAE: Acesoft Technologies FZCO, IFZA Business Park, Dubai
  • USA: Acesoft Solutions Inc., Lewes, Delaware

We are an ISO 9001:2015 certified company. That means quality, process discipline and accountability are part of how every engagement runs, not something we add later.

From a Focused Delivery Team to a Full-Scale Partner

Acesoft began as a focused IT delivery team. Over 16 years, we grew into a full-scale solutions and services provider. Our clients range from global enterprises to product-driven companies.

Along the way, we learned something important. An empty seat is never free. When a critical role stays unfilled, sprints slip, launches move, SLAs get missed and the rest of the team burns out covering the gap. Many of the roles that matter most are niche technology positions that generic job searches cannot close.

That insight shapes how we work today. We keep a strong talent pool, and we search locally first. We find skilled people within each client's own country, whether that is India, the UAE or the USA, and widen the search globally only when the role truly requires it. The result is faster hiring, smoother onboarding and teams that fit the local business context.

One Partner, Four Ways to Deliver

Acesoft's services are organised around four pillars. Clients can use one, or combine several as their needs grow.

1. Talent & Workforce

For companies that need the right people, fast.

  • IT staff augmentation for short-term projects and long-term capacity
  • Contract and permanent hiring
  • Recruitment Process Outsourcing (RPO)
  • Specialist search for niche and hard-to-fill technology roles

Our pre-vetted, project-ready experts cover cloud, AI/ML, DevOps, enterprise software and emerging technologies. Flexible engagement models: time and material, SOW-based staffing, onshore, offshore or hybrid.

2. Employer of Record (EOR) & Workforce Management

For companies that want to hire and manage talent without building local HR infrastructure.

  • Onboarding, contracts and documentation
  • Payroll and full lifecycle support
  • Timesheets and invoicing
  • Background verification
  • Cross-border coordination

3. Technology & Project Delivery

For companies that need a partner to build and run technology, end to end.

  • Enterprise Software Development: scalable, high-performance applications built on distributed systems, microservices and cloud-native architecture.
  • Cloud Computing: cloud strategy, migration, cloud-native builds and managed services on AWS, Azure and GCP, with CI/CD, autoscaling and secure continuity built in.
  • Data & AI-Driven Business Insights: real-time analytics, data pipelines and AI model integration using tools like Spark, Kafka and Flink, including Vision AI with edge deployment and real-time inference.
  • Engineering Services (Embedded & Semiconductor): ASIC design, RTL development, SoC-centric development, embedded firmware and edge-to-cloud integration, aligned with industry-standard EDA tools and design flows.
  • Cybersecurity, QA and testing: security integrated into the architecture, and quality built into every release.
  • Custom applications, managed services and remote infrastructure management.

4. Business & Advisory

For companies that want to spend smarter on people, vendors and technology.

  • Strategic sourcing of resources and vendors
  • Vendor evaluation
  • Spend analysis and cost optimisation recommendations for resources and IT infrastructure
  • Contract management

Staffing Support for Global Capability Centers (GCCs)

Global Capability Centers need to scale fast, often with niche skills that are hard to find. Acesoft supports GCCs with the talent they need to build and grow their teams:

  • Specialist search for niche technology roles in cloud, AI/ML, data, DevOps, cybersecurity, and embedded and semiconductor engineering
  • Contract and permanent hiring to stand up new teams or expand existing ones
  • Flexible staff augmentation to cover project peaks without long-term overhead
  • Local-first sourcing from our talent pool, with a global search only when a role requires it
  • Fast onboarding, so new hires add value from day one
